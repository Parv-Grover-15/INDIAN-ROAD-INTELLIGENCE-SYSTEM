THE CHALLENGE

Problem Statement & Motivation

🚨 Unstructured Traffic
Extreme vehicle heterogeneity (auto-rickshaws, bikes, buses, trucks, pedestrians).
Lack of lane markings makes classical edge/line detection fail completely.
High occlusion and scale diversity across urban & rural roads.
⚠️ Severe Road Hazards
Unmonitored potholes and structural cracks cause thousands of accidents annually.
Manual road inspection is slow, expensive, and fails to scale.
Delay in detection accelerates severe pavement degradation.
💡 Our AI Solution
Unified multi-model pipeline combining detection, segmentation, and severity grading.
Automated Euclidean spatial proximity hazard vector calculation.
Single-loading image & video analytics dashboard with instant JSON reports.
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Decoupled Adapter Framework

🏗️ Architectural Design Principles
Decoupled Model Adapters: Insulates third-party repository code from application logic via BaseModelAdapter contract.
Graceful Degradation: Missing model weights bypass gracefully without interrupting active components.
Single-Load Model Persistence: Models load EXACTLY ONCE upon initialization to preserve video FPS.
Normalized Contract: Standardizes bounding boxes, confidence, and mask shapes across models.
🧩 Integrated Model Stack
1. Generic Objects: YOLOv8 (COCO) → Cars, Motorcycles, Buses, Trucks, Pedestrians.
2. Road Damage: RoadScanCV YOLO → Potholes, Cracks, Manholes.
3. Drivable Area: IDD ResNet50 U-Net → Binary drivable surface mask.
4. Pothole Severity: iWatchRoad Heuristic → Area-based Low/Mod/High severity.
5. Traffic Signs: YOLOv8s → 56 Indian sign categories (Optional).
ANALYSIS ENGINE

Spatial Risk & Road Hazard Indicator (0–100)

🎯 Spatial Proximity Risk Engine
Computes Euclidean distance between road user centers and hazard bounding boxes.
CRITICAL alert triggered when distance < 75px between vulnerable users (pedestrians) and severe potholes.
Renders visual interaction risk lines directly on the output frame.
📊 Hazard Score Formula (0–100)
Potholes: High (+15), Mod (+8), Low (+3).
Road Users: Pedestrians (+7), Vehicles (+2).
Proximity Penalty: +10 per critical hazard interaction.
Categorical Scale:
🟢 0–24: LOW | 🟡 25–54: MODERATE | 🟠 55–79: HIGH | 🔴 80–100: VERY HIGH
